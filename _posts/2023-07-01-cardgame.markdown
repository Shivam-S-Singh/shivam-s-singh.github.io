## Overview
I had the opportunity recently to solve a personal issue I was having with my Nintendo 3DS using Wireshark. 

I wanted to backup the game data from my MicroSD card on my 3DS to my PC. The 3DS has a file transfer application called microSD Management which lets you access and transfer your files from a Windows 7 or 8.1 PC with Network Sharing enabled.
